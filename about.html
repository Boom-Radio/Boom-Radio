ABOUT BOOM

Who is BOOM ?

Officially launched in 2012, BOOM Radio is a student-run, not-for-profit radio station at North Metropolitan TAFE in Leederville. We bring students together through broadcasting, local music and creativity.

What drives us

Our Mission. . . . . . .

BOOM Radio brings students together through broadcasting, local music and creativity, giving young voices a place to be heard.

01

Empower Students

Give students hands-on experience in broadcasting, audio production and media management.

02

Build Community

Bring students, listeners and local communities together through shared stories and conversations.

03

Celebrate Creativity

Encourage fresh ideas, student-led shows and creative expression through radio.

04

Promote Local Music

Showcase emerging artists and share the sounds of Perth's local music scene.
